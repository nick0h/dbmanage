Outlook Setup Notes
  • For Microsoft 365 / Outlook, use your full email as the login and your account password or app password.
  • Outlook SMTP defaults to smtp.office365.com on port 587 with TLS enabled.
  • If you see SmtpClientAuthentication is disabled, your Microsoft 365 admin must enable Authenticated SMTP for your mailbox. See Microsoft's guide.
  • In Exchange Online PowerShell, an admin can run: Set-CASMailbox -Identity you@company.com -SmtpClientAuthenticationDisabled $false
  • If your organization blocks SMTP, use Gmail with an app password instead, or ask IT to enable SMTP AUTH.
  • Enable "Use these site settings" and save before sending a test email.
